# Gastromasa project rules

- Publish authorized site revisions by pushing `main`; Vercel deploys automatically.
- The site is temporarily Turkish-only. Keep all 14 existing language datasets, biographies, dictionaries and the stored language-menu template. Restore multilingual publication only when the user explicitly requests the final multilingual release. The feature flag is `multilingualEnabled` in `i18n.js`.
- Chef/person names, restaurant/organisation names and venue names are proper names: NEVER translate them when changing languages. Canonical spellings and speaker venues are in `proper-names.json` and `program.en.json`. Preserve them in every program dataset and use `notranslate` markup on their rendered fields. Job descriptions and generic interface labels may be translated. Do not silently translate brand names such as Disfrutar, Table, BACHOUR, HIDE, Wing, Geranium or Portrait Milano.
- Before the final multilingual release, audit existing biography translations for transliterated names and translated restaurant aliases. Preserve original entities with placeholders when translating full paragraphs; avoid blind word replacement in running prose.
- External press coverage cards live under Basında Biz and are grouped by publication origin into Yerel Mecralar and Yabancı Mecralar, independently of article language. Euronews (including its Turkish edition) and Kıbrıs Postası are in Yabancı Mecralar. Preserve archived articles' actual publication dates. Use source-verified images, titles and dates; never invent publication dates. Keep original foreign headlines in `press.json` even when showing a Turkish rendering.
- Keep OneSignal and cron secrets in Vercel environment variables. Never commit or request secret values in chat.

- Sponsor cards now open stand details (superseding the earlier non-clickable logo design). Stand data is in `stand-directory.json`. Never present assigned digital codes or suggested brand-family matches as official PDF evidence. Preserve conflict notes and do not invent locations for firms absent from the PDF. See `STAND-ESLESTIRME.md`.

- Stand popups use a simple presentation: logo, name, stand, hall, floor-plan name and map link. Per user revision, omit PDF source badges/links, suggested-match labels and explanatory notes from popups. Keep provenance/conflict details in the data and audit document; keep assigned-code clarification in the floor-plan legend.

- All 71 sponsor/partner cards show one Turkish introductory paragraph from `partner-profiles.json` above any existing stand details. Keep website/source metadata and access exceptions in that data and `PARTNER-TANITIM-KAYNAKLARI.md`; do not restore PDF badges or source notes in the popup. Profiles use original Turkish summaries, not long copied passages. Preserve brand names. Zest is Zest Porcelain; Keramikos is the Urla brand by Kerem Alsan.

- The home Bilet Al button opens the local `#ticketDialog` platform selector (`tickets.js`). Keep all four user-provided event links: Biletix, Passo, Mobilet and Bubilet. Logos are stored locally under `assets/tickets`, rendered white on navy rows inside a white dialog. Do not restore the old Carrd ticket redirect. Provider links open their event checkout pages in a new tab.

- `#haberler` is the dedicated Gastromasa news section, separate from external press coverage in Basında Biz. `news.json` stores 15 entries (the initial latest five plus the ten immediately preceding them) verified against https://www.gastromasa.com/news/ and the official WordPress posts API, in source order with original publication dates, original titles and locally stored source images. `news.js` opens Turkish editorial summaries in the app and links to each original story. Preserve proper names. Do not silently replace publication dates with import dates or claim automatic feed syncing; this is a curated snapshot.

- Show the existing `#haberler` section on the home page immediately after the hero’s Rakamlarla Gastromasa statistics. Keep its menu/deep link and shared article dialogs; do not duplicate the news cards. Other routes continue to show only their selected content.


- The footer includes compact Instagram, Facebook, X and YouTube icon links to the user-provided Gastromasa accounts. Newsletter signup is postponed at the user’s request; do not publish a newsletter form or email endpoint until requested again.

- For press cards, use the existing Gastromasa stage photograph when the source has no usable news image or only an author photo / publication placeholder; do not show author portraits as article covers. Deduplicate canonical URLs and repeated headlines within the same publication before adding cards. The KTB print link was excluded by explicit user request; do not add it. See `BASIN-EKLEME-NOTLARI.md`.

- Production static output is generated by `node scripts/build-site.cjs` into ignored `public/`. Add new public files to its explicit allowlist; never copy backend code, internal notes or environment files into it. Preserve CSP compatibility and run `node --test tests/*.cjs` before publishing security changes.

- Basında Biz now has 50 unique press cards (37 local, 13 international) plus an Instagram feature for reel `DebJFE4oIi3`. The user explicitly chose Instagram embed with music resuming when the video dialog closes, accepting that iframe playback completion cannot be detected. `press-video.js` loads the iframe only on click and calls `SiteMusic.hold/release`; preserve this behavior and the narrow Instagram CSP frame allowance. Do not use expiring Instagram CDN video URLs.

- The Instagram feature is on the home page immediately after `.cover-gallery`, inside `.hero`, rather than in Basında Biz. Preserve the cover's full landscape aspect ratio with `object-fit: contain`; keep descriptive text outside the image so brand marks are not obscured. The current cover is the user-supplied, unmodified 1888×1048 PNG with “11th Edition”, stored as `assets/press/instagram-user-cover.png`.

- Capacity/performance revision: public JSON is minified by `scripts/build-site.cjs`; source JSON remains readable. `data-loader.js` shares JSON requests, uses a deployment revision (`62`) and bounded retries. When changing public data, bump its revision together with index script/style query versions to avoid stale browser data. Basında Biz cards are now rendered on first section visit from `press.json` by `press.js`, not duplicated as static card markup in index.html. Keep all 50 verified articles and grouped dates/images. Partner/news detail data loads on demand. Preserve the final worker no-cache header override.
- Optimized hero/gallery WebP assets are under `assets/optimized/`. The user's video cover is served as a pixel-identical lossless WebP there; keep the original PNG under `assets/press/` and full 1888×1048 composition. Performance figures and verification limits are documented in `PERFORMANS-INCELEMESI.md`; do not claim 2,000-user capacity based on small smoke tests.
